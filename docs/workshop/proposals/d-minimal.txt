[h1]Install[/h1]
Subscribing alone does nothing. Then:
[olist]
[*]Mods: enable [b]PZ_Optimization[/b] and press [b]Copy the command[/b].
[*]Paste it into PowerShell (Windows) or a terminal (Linux, macOS).
[*]Quit the game; start it again; disable the mod.
[/olist]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xD3I/PZ_Optimization/master/docs/workshop/images/40-install.gif[/img]

[h1]Not supported[/h1]
[b]Other mods and multiplayer are untested and unsupported.[/b] Use them at your own risk. If anything looks wrong, pick the "Stock game" preset in Options > PZ Optimization and tell us on Discord.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Driving p99 frame time 19 -> 8 ms; storms 83 -> 188 fps; loading about twice as fast.
[*]Hordes on every CPU core.
[*]Smooth zoom, steady car camera, lag-free UI.
[*]FSR upscaling, dynamic resolution and a low-end preset.
[*]Opt-in graphics: per-pixel lighting, real sun and moon shadows, god rays, reflections, glass car windows, colour grading, HDR.
[*]F9 performance overlay, in-menu updates, one-click uninstall.
[/list]
